### Practical English Grammar
Status: Idea

**Goal:** Help learners construct more natural English sentences.

#### Connectors
- Learn connectors such as because, although, however,
  therefore, and unless.
- Understand when and how to use each connector.
- Practice connecting sentences.
- Complete sentences with the appropriate connector.

#### Verb Tenses
- Practice present, past, and future tenses.
- Understand the differences between simple,
  continuous, and perfect forms.
- Transform sentences between tenses.
- Identify the correct tense based on context.
- Practice affirmative, negative, and question forms.

#### Open Questions
- Should exercises use software development contexts?
- Should learners write sentences or select answers?
- Should grammar topics have progressive difficulty?
- Could saved vocabulary be reused in grammar exercises?
